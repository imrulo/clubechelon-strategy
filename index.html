Key idea for you as the owner: people do not fall in love with a DJ name, they fall in love with a habit and a place. The Latin after-party gives Belgrade dancers and friends one clear option every time the regular party ends: “after 2:00, we go to Echelon”.

This page is designed so you can play with a few numbers and quickly see how many people and how much spend per person you need for the concept to be clearly profitable.
